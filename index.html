About Me

Hey! I'm Fariha Alam, a Class 9 Science student at Shaheed Police Smrity College, Mirpur, Dhaka. I'm a creative and curious learner with a growing interest in information technology and computer science.

I love exploring how computers work, discovering new technologies, and learning the basics of coding. I'm still at the beginning of my journey, but every new concept gives me another reason to keep exploring.
